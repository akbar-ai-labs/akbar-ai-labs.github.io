When AI isn't sure: copy patterns

Adapt the words to your product. The structure is what matters: what's known, what's missing, what to do next.

Missing data

  • "I found {n} of {total} {items}. {missing item} is missing, so I can't give a full answer yet."
  • "I couldn't find {thing} in {source}. Do you want to add it, or check another source?"

Low confidence

  • "This is likely, based on {evidence}. Please check {source} before acting on it."
  • "I'm not sure about this one. Here's what I found and where it came from."

Conflicting sources

  • "{source A} says {value A}, but {source B} says {value B}. Which one should I trust?"

Out of scope

  • "That's outside what I can do here. You can {alternative action} or ask {team/person}."

Numbers

  • Never show a calculated number if an input is missing. Say: "I can't calculate {metric} because {input} is missing."

Next-step buttons that work well

  • "Check the source" · "Add the missing data" · "Ask a colleague" · "Try a narrower question"

Words to prefer

Instead of Say
73% confidence Likely / Not sure / Not enough data
Error occurred I couldn't find …
Results may be inaccurate Please check {specific thing} before acting
