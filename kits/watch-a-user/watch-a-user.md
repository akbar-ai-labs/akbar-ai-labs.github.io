# The 20-minute user test

1. **Pick one task** (2 min): one thing a real user should be able to do, like "Export last month's invoices".
2. **Find one real person** who matches your user. A colleague from another team is better than no one.
3. **Set it up** (2 min): "I'm testing the product, not you. Please think out loud."
4. **Give the task and stay quiet** (10 min). Don't help, don't explain. Write down:
   - where they hesitate,
   - what they click first,
   - the exact words they say.
5. **Ask three questions** (5 min):
   - "What did you expect to happen there?"
   - "What was the hardest part?"
   - "If you could change one thing, what would it be?"
6. **Write one line** (1 min): the single biggest problem you saw. Fix that first.

Do this with three people and you'll find most of the big problems.
