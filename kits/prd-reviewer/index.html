PRD reviewer

Checks your PRD for 9 gaps before engineering sees it. You get a verdict, a scorecard, and the questions to answer. It never rewrites your document.

Download Claude skill (.zip) Download prompt (.md)

What it checks

#AreaPasses when…
1Problem and evidenceThe problem is in the user's words and backed by data or real examples.
2Target usersA specific segment is named, with the situation they're in.
3Goals and non-goalsGoals are stated, plus at least one thing this work won't do.
4Success metricsEach metric has a baseline, a target and a time frame.
5Scope and requirementsRequirements are testable and prioritised.
6Edge cases and failure statesEmpty, error, loading, permission and offline states are covered.
7Risks and dependenciesEach risk and dependency has an owner.
8Rollout and measurementThere's a release plan and a way to measure the metrics.
9Open questionsEach unresolved decision has an owner and a date.

For AI features it also asks: what happens when the AI is wrong, how users can check an answer, and what one use costs at scale.

How to use it

In Claude (as a skill)

  1. Download prd-reviewer-skill.zip.
  2. In Claude, open Settings → Capabilities → Skills and upload the ZIP. (Skills need to be available on your Claude plan.)
  3. Share a PRD in any chat and ask: "Review this PRD."

In Claude Code

  1. Unzip it into ~/.claude/skills/ (or your project's .claude/skills/), then ask Claude Code to review a PRD file.

In any other AI assistant

  1. Open prd-reviewer-prompt.md, paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot, and add your PRD at the end.

Tips

  • Run it before your review meeting, not after.
  • Fix the gaps yourself. The reviewer's job is to ask, not to write for you.
  • Change the nine checks to match your team's PRD template. It's a plain text file.
