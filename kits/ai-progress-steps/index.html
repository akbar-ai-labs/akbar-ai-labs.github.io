AI progress steps: naming guide

Replace "Thinking…" with steps people understand.

Rules

  1. Use the user's words: "Reading 3 contracts", not "Retrieving documents from vector store".
  2. 3 to 5 steps: enough to show progress, few enough to read.
  3. Show the step, not every token.
  4. Say roughly how long if it takes more than a few seconds.
  5. Show what was used once it's done ("Read 3 contracts, 1 policy").

Step states

✓ done · ● in progress · ○ not started · ⚠ needs your input

Ready-made step names

Task Steps
Summarise documents Reading {n} documents → Finding the key points → Writing the summary
Answer from data Finding the right data → Checking it's complete → Calculating {metric} → Writing the answer
Compare against policy Reading {items} → Finding {terms} → Comparing with your policy → Flagging differences
Draft an email Reading the thread → Picking the main points → Drafting in your tone
Research Searching {n} sources → Reading the best matches → Checking facts → Writing the report
Review a document Reading the document → Checking {n} areas → Listing what to fix
